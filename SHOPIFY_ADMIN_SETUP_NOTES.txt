================================================================================
PRÜV SHOPIFY STORE - ADMIN PANEL SETUP GUIDE (OPTION 2: BUNDLE UPSELL)
================================================================================

This guide explains step-by-step how to create your bundle kit product in the 
Shopify Admin Panel and connect it to the new Above-the-Fold Bundle Upsell block.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: CREATE YOUR BUNDLE PRODUCT IN SHOPIFY ADMIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Log in to your Shopify Admin (e.g., your-store.myshopify.com/admin).
2. On the left sidebar, click on "Products", then click the "Add product" button.
3. Fill in the product details:
   - Title:
     Example: "Prüv Complete Growth Ritual (2-Pack Duo)"
     or "Hair Growth Elixir + Scalp Massager Kit"
   
   - Media / Images:
     Upload a photo showing the bundle (e.g. both bottles or kit box).
   
   - Pricing (Crucial for automated savings display):
     * Price: Enter the discounted bundle price (e.g., ₹1,499)
     * Compare-at price: Enter the full regular total (e.g., ₹1,798)
     -> Our theme automatically detects this difference and displays 
        "SAVE ₹299" or "SAVE 15%" on the upsell card!
   
   - Inventory & Status:
     * Set Status to "Active".
     * Under "Publishing", ensure "Online Store" is checked.
     * Track quantity as appropriate for your inventory.

4. Click "Save".


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: CONNECT THE BUNDLE IN THE SHOPIFY THEME EDITOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In Shopify Admin, go to "Online Store" -> "Themes".
2. Next to your active theme, click the green "Customize" button.
3. At the top of the editor, click the page dropdown (it says "Home page") and 
   select "Products" -> "Default product".
4. Look at the left sidebar under "Product information" (the first section).
   You will see the Above-The-Fold hierarchy in this exact order:
     1. Title (Product Name)
     2. Product descriptor
     3. Price
     4. Rating (Reviews)
     5. Key benefit line
     6. Variant picker (Size)
     7. Quantity selector
     8. Buy buttons (Add to Cart - Dominating CTA)
     9. Bundle upsell card  <--- CLICK THIS BLOCK
5. In the block settings on the right sidebar:
   - Click "Select product" under "Bundle product (from Admin)".
   - Choose the bundle product you created in Step 1.
   - (Optional) Customize the Card heading (e.g., "UPGRADE & SAVE MORE").
   - (Optional) Customize the Savings badge (e.g., "SAVE 15%" or "BEST VALUE").
   - (Optional) Customize the Button label (e.g., "Add Bundle").
6. Click "Save" in the top-right corner.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: HOW TO CUSTOMIZE DESCRIPTOR & BENEFIT LINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can customize the other above-the-fold elements anytime in Theme Editor:

1. Product Descriptor:
   - Click the "Product descriptor" block in the left sidebar.
   - Enter your preferred subtitle (e.g. "Advanced Scalp & Follicle Energizing Elixir").
   - (Alternatively, you can leave it blank and it will read from your product's 
     built-in Subtitle metafield).

2. Key Benefit Line:
   - Click the "Key benefit line" block in the left sidebar.
   - Change the text (e.g. "Clinically Proven • Visible Growth in 84 Days • 100% Water-Based").

3. Reviews / Stars:
   - Displays 5 copper stars (4.9 / 5.0 • 474 Reviews).
   - Clicking it automatically smooth-scrolls down directly to the customer reviews section!
